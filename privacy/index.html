Privacy.

OKNO Design is a sole trader based in the UK. This page explains what happens to the details you give when you order something.

What is collected

When you submit an order form: your name, email address, delivery address, the item you ordered, and an order number. Nothing else. There is no account, no newsletter and no payment details on this site.

Why

To email you a payment link, to post the work to you, and to sort out any problem with the order. That is it.

How long it is kept

Order details are deleted after one month. Email is kept only for as long as it takes to finish the order and deal with any follow-up.

Who else sees it

  • Google — the order form is processed by a private Google Apps Script and the order details arrive by email.
  • Cloudflare — Turnstile checks the form for spam and bots.
  • GitHub Pages — hosts this website.
  • Monzo — sends the payment link if you choose to pay by bank transfer.

Your details are never sold, rented or shared for marketing.

Cookies and tracking

There is no analytics, no advertising and no profiling on this site. Fonts are served by Google, and Turnstile may set its own technical cookie to tell humans from bots.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete what is held about you, and you can object to it being used. Email okno.orders@gmail.com. If you are not satisfied with the answer, you can complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office at ico.org.uk.

Contact

OKNO Design, okno.orders@gmail.com.
